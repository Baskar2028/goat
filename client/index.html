THE ORIGIN

Built by GoatedHeisen

This workspace was engineered by GoatedHeisen, the visionary developer behind hit Roblox experiences like Telugu Karaoke and Tamil are you lonely.

Curating the perfect audio atmosphere for these games was a massive undertaking. Adding songs meant preparing, pitching, correcting tempos, and uploading hundreds of tracks manually. It was a painstaking, one-man job that took countless hours and bottlenecked development.

GoatedHeisen built this tool to solve that exact problem. By mathematically automating pitch correction, tempo synchronization, and direct Roblox Open Cloud uploads, this tool ensures developers can focus on building incredible games instead of battling audio limitations. Built by a creator, for creators.
